# ADR-0013: publish an existing report as a transparent mechanism story

The owner selected Harness² for the first VV Frontier publication and requested reuse of the saved research, without another provider run. Keep the original report, plan, approval, usage and knowledge immutable. Store the reader-facing editorial, decisions, evidence excerpts, limits and unknown costs as a separate review revision. Manual rubric scores remain unknown until supplied by a human.

Apply the existing reader infographic to reviewed legacy reports with a three-step process flow. Preserve the specialized replay rendering. Numeric comparisons still require a supported baseline/result pair; maxima from different benchmarks cannot become a common-scale bar chart or a synthetic zero baseline.

Without such a comparison, show the full three-step mechanism and the reason no chart is drawn. Encode a native-width 1080 px H.264 animation as three scenes of three seconds, highlighting the active step. Preserve full text, source URLs and an explicit schematic/no-measurement caption. Reject an unreadably oversized frame rather than shrinking or truncating it. The motion expresses sequence, never measured throughput, improvement or elapsed experiment time. Existing numeric comparison animations retain their rendering path. Reader media use the VV Frontier heading.

The same renderer serves future reports; there is no Harness-specific graphics branch. Frozen publication packages still require a fresh owner approval in Telegram bound to the exact content and channel before a real scheduler tick may publish. Preparing this editorial or rendering its media makes no provider calls and does not itself authorize a post.

Verification covers reviewed legacy routing, full steps and sources, rejection of oversized layouts, no fabricated comparison from deltas, actual H.264 decode and retained native-resolution fidelity, plus the existing suite.
