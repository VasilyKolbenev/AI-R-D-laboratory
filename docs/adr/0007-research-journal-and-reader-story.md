# ADR-0007: evidence-linked research journal and an applied reader story

Status: local implementation requested by the owner on 2026-10-21. No new research invocation or benchmark is authorized by this increment. No alpha gate is accepted.

## Product direction

The laboratory is a fixed research product. An incoming AI news item, paper or technology is evaluated for the reader's work or project. The output should let a reader understand the practical relevance in about five minutes. A source is not implicitly a proposal to modify the laboratory itself. Public conclusions identify the application, audience, conditions, evidence and requirements. The default workflow no longer produces an internal laboratory adoption verdict.

Keep this sequence: working problem and audience → mechanism and baseline → author results with conditions → limitations → setup and resources → practical choice criteria. Separate author measurements, editorial interpretation and unavailable data. An illustrative diagram may show a mechanism or a hypothetical route; it must not impersonate a measured performance comparison.

## Journal and quality

Add append-only research_review_revisions linked to the existing run. A revision records research decisions, the claim or comparison they concern, reasons, evidence references, boundaries and next checks. Evidence references identify provenance (saved report, local run record, editorial assessment or missing evidence), a locator, optional public URL, excerpt and limitations. A URL is not an independent verification.

This increment retrospectively annotates the completed Dream-RSI report. It does not reconstruct hidden agent steps or claim that newly written journal entries were captured during the earlier run. Original run output, approval, dispatch context and usage are preserved. The corrected editorial story is a separate revision, derived from the existing report, with no extra provider invocation.

The simple rubric uses five criteria, each null or 0/1/2: task and audience; mechanism and baseline; evidence and conditions; applicability and cost; communication and next step. Scored criteria require justification; a maximum score requires an evidence reference. Null remains unknown. An editorial total is not a truth probability, technology score, independent scientific replication or permission to publish.

## Costs and persistence

Show primary and explicitly related error runs separately, then known aggregate usage and execution durations. Cached input remains a subset of the CLI's reported input. Do not derive a monetary charge or API request count from token usage. Manual money/time notes remain separate; unknown is not zero. Development and editorial effort are outside observed research-run usage unless the owner records them.

Backup schema 5 adds journal history. Restore supports schemas 1–5, validates history/reference integrity and remains atomic and empty-destination-only. Older backups do not acquire fabricated journals. Operational Telegram handles and credentials remain excluded.

## Visual delivery

The result page displays the mechanism diagram directly, with readable steps, play/pause/reset and reduced-motion support. For the Dream-RSI case, the diagram compares route selection on the same hypothetical recorded tree, leaves unknown branches unknown and shows that new outcomes require a later real online run. Route movement represents traversal order, never measured speed or a free new execution.

An optional editorial.comparisons list holds up to three explicitly entered numeric comparisons, each with units, labels, conditions and references to existing evidence. Values must be finite and nonnegative. Graphs use separate zero-based scales and show the conditions; they are not mined heuristically from unrelated numbers in prose. A comparison may be omitted when evidence is insufficient. Telegram receives the evidence-linked graph when available and a separate mechanism animation alongside the applied post draft. Publication remains a manual action by the owner.

Verification uses fake providers, isolated data and browser contexts. The real Dream-RSI report is annotated and reformatted without a new model run. Re-sending its revised presentation to the paired owner is an output operation, not a research retry.
