# VV Frontier · v0.21.0-alpha.1

First corpus increment: import published Drift Inspector EMNLP articles and claims,
recompute the canonical topic trajectories and explore the underlying sources
inside the existing workbench at `/workbench/#corpus`.

The optional dataset contains 6,517 papers and 18,293 external claims. Its balanced
analysis sample contains 4,488 papers (748/year), 16,576 assignments and 80 topics
plus noise. All 486 topic/year shares are checked against the author's rounded
snapshot. The UI separates the full catalog, analysis sample and missing coverage.
Click a year to inspect exact numerator/denominator papers, original abstracts and
imported claims with explicit absent-quote and unverified-support labels.

Corpus storage is an immutable sidecar included in full operational backups;
ordinary research JSON export remains separate. Data download and Parquet
conversion are explicit optional steps; the working app retains its existing
lightweight dependencies. [Import instructions](../research/drift-corpus.md).

No new extraction, embedding, clustering or model research is performed. Existing
research approvals, budgets, history, Telegram and publication gates are retained.
Corpus-directed automatic research and corpus-derived Telegram posts are future
increments, not capabilities of this release.
